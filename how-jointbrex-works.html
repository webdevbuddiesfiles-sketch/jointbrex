What the Capsule Does

JointBrex is a supplement and not a drug. It supplies turmeric extracts, coleus and a black pepper extract meant to raise curcumin absorption, and the seller positions it to support joint comfort, mobility and flexibility.* See the ingredients and the benefits.

Four Steps

Follow these with the daily routine beside you.

1

Read the label

Study the directions, amounts and cautions on your bottle.

2

Ask about medicine

Show your pharmacist the label if you take any medicine.

3

One capsule

The seller says one capsule daily.

4

Keep moving

Pair it with gentle, regular activity and judge it over weeks.

Tips

Small habits make it stick. See joint-friendly daily habits.

  • Take it at the same time each day.
  • Follow your bottle on food and water.
  • Store as the label directs, away from heat.
  • Keep it out of reach of children.
