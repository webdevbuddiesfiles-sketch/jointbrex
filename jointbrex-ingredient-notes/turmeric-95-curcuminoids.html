About Turmeric, 95% Curcuminoids

In short: A concentrated curcuminoid extract.*

This second turmeric extract is standardised to 95% curcuminoids, the compounds studied for joint comfort, at 30 mg per capsule. Together with the 4:1 extract it makes turmeric the main part of the formula.

See the ingredients and safety and interactions.

Standardised Curcuminoids

Standardised means the extract is adjusted so that 95% of it is curcuminoids. See why curcumin is paired with black pepper.

What Research Shows

Studies on joint comfort are encouraging but mixed. See the benefits.

Main Caution

Curcuminoid research in joints is promising but mixed, and doses and products vary a lot. It carries the same cautions as the other turmeric extract, so avoid doubling up on turmeric supplements. See safety and interactions.
