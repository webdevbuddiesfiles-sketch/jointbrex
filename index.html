05 · The formula

Inside JointBrex: Four Active Extracts, Amounts Listed

Tap an item for the short version.*

Turmeric Root Extract 4:1 (75 mg)

In short: The largest listed amount.*

Turmeric (Curcuma longa) is the golden root of curries and traditional medicine. JointBrex lists a 4:1 root extract, about four parts of root concentrated into one, at 75 mg per capsule, the largest amount on its list.

Open the note

Turmeric Extract, 95% Curcuminoids (30 mg)

In short: A concentrated curcuminoid extract.*

This second turmeric extract is standardised to 95% curcuminoids, the compounds studied for joint comfort, at 30 mg per capsule. Together with the 4:1 extract it makes turmeric the main part of the formula.

Open the note

Coleus Forskohlii Extract (15 mg)

In short: A traditional root with a drug-like compound.*

Coleus forskohlii is a plant used in Ayurvedic practice, and its extract contains forskolin. JointBrex lists 15 mg per capsule, and the reference says it may support circulation and metabolic balance.

Open the note

Black Pepper Extract, BioPerine (5 mg)

In short: Added to raise absorption.*

Black pepper fruit extract, sold under the trademark BioPerine, supplies piperine, which can raise how much curcumin the body absorbs. JointBrex lists 5 mg per capsule.

Open the note

Capsule and Other Ingredients

In short: What holds the extracts together.*

The other ingredients are microcrystalline cellulose, gelatin and magnesium stearate. Gelatin forms the capsule shell, and the others help with flow and stability.

Open the note

125 mg of actives, every amount listed

The seller lists four active extracts totalling 125 mg per capsule, with each amount shown, which makes the formula easier to judge than a proprietary blend. Compare it with the ingredients page and read safety and interactions.

11 · Questions

JointBrex FAQ: Ten Clear Answers

What is JointBrex?

JointBrex is a daily capsule supplement sold to support joint mobility, flexibility and everyday comfort as part of an active lifestyle.* It is not a medicine and not a treatment for arthritis or any other condition.

What is in JointBrex?

Per capsule the seller lists turmeric root extract 4:1 (75 mg), turmeric extract with 95% curcuminoids (30 mg), coleus forskohlii extract (15 mg) and black pepper extract, BioPerine (5 mg), plus microcrystalline cellulose, gelatin and magnesium stearate. See the ingredients.

How do I take it?

The seller says one capsule daily. Follow the directions on your own bottle. See how JointBrex works and how to use it.

How many capsules are in a bottle?

The bottle photos show a 60-count bottle. At one capsule a day that is about 60 days, but the seller lists 2, 3 and 6 bottles as 60, 90 and 180 day supplies, which is 30 days a bottle. Check the directions and count on your own label.

Is it a treatment for arthritis or joint pain?

No. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, including arthritis, and it does not replace medical care or prescribed medicine.

Who should talk to a doctor first?

Anyone pregnant or nursing, anyone under 18, anyone who takes blood thinners, blood pressure medicine or other prescriptions, and anyone with gallbladder, liver or kidney problems or surgery planned. See safety and interactions.

Can it affect my medicines?

Yes, possibly. The black pepper extract can raise the levels of some medicines, turmeric may add to blood thinners and coleus can lower blood pressure, so show the label to your pharmacist before starting.

Is it vegetarian?

No. The capsule contains gelatin, which comes from animal collagen, so the product is not vegetarian or vegan, even though the seller calls the formula natural and plant-based.

What do the bundles cost?

Two bottles are $79 each ($158 plus $9.99 shipping), three are $69 each ($207, free shipping) and six are $49 each ($294, free shipping). See pricing and confirm every total at checkout.

How does the guarantee work?

It is a 60-day money-back guarantee. The seller says to contact support within 60 days of purchase, that you may return empty bottles and that contact details are on the bottle. See the 60-day guarantee and the refund policy.
